How it works

  1. We build a preview

    You see your own site, with your menu, hours and photos, before you pay for anything.

  2. You approve and sign

    A one-page written agreement sets the price and what is included. A deposit starts the final work.

  3. We launch it

    Your site goes live on your own domain. Pick a monthly plan if you want us to keep it up to date.

What we offer

Prices are quoted per project and agreed in writing before any work starts.

Website design and build

A custom, mobile-friendly site for one location: design, content setup, your domain connected and launch. Paid once, in two parts.

Care plan, monthly

Hosting, domain renewal help and small updates, like a new menu or new hours. Optional, and you can cancel it.

Payments are taken by invoice or payment link through Stripe. Terms, including cancellation and refunds, are set out in each written agreement.
